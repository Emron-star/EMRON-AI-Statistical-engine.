Questions

Is this an official Deriv product?

No. It is an independent tool that connects to your Deriv account through Deriv's official API.

Where does my token go?

You paste a scoped personal access token. It is used to ask Deriv for your Options accounts and a one-time WebSocket address, and is kept in page memory only. Disconnecting, or closing the tab, drops it.

Does any strategy guarantee profit?

No. Every Deriv contract is priced with a margin, so over many trades the expected result is a loss of roughly that margin on everything staked, whatever the staking plan. Recovery staking changes the shape of results (many small wins, occasional large losses), not the expectation. Historical replay and forward observations can reject bad candidates, but neither demonstrates guaranteed future profit.

Demo or real?

Start on demo. Real accounts need a stop loss above zero and a typed confirmation before trading starts.

Trading options carries a high risk of losing money. This tool is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by Deriv. Nothing here is financial advice.
